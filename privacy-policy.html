Privacy Policy for Rs Music

App: Rs Music  ·  Package: com.rsmusic.rs_music  ·  Version: 1.0.0  · 
Effective date: November 20, 2026  ·  Developer: Rohit Kumar / RS STUDIOZ

In short: Rs Music has no internet permission and no accounts. We do not collect, transmit, store or share your music, playlists, settings or any personal data. Everything stays on your Android device.

1. Who we are

Rs Music is a personal music player developed by Rohit Kumar / RS STUDIOZ. This policy explains what data the app handles on your device. It applies to version 1.0.0 and later.

2. Information we collect

None. Rs Music does not ask you to sign in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash-reporting tools, tracking libraries, or cloud services inside the app. Your listening is not monitored or monetised.

3. Data stored on your device

Music library metadataRead from Android MediaStore to display your songs, albums and folders
Playlists and favouritesStored locally in an app-private database
Settings and themesStored locally on your device
Exported backup filesPlain JSON files saved only when you tap export

The developer has no access to any of this data. Nothing is uploaded, synced, backed up to a server, or shared with a third party.

About network access: Rs Music does not declare android.permission.INTERNET, so it cannot send data anywhere. It does declare ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which only allows it to check whether a network is available. No information is read from the network or transmitted.

4. Permissions explained

INTERNETNot requested
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGERead your local songs only
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (old Android)Tag and artwork writes on older devices
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow playback controls
FOREGROUND_SERVICE / WAKE_LOCKKeep music playing with the screen off
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEConnectivity check only - no data sent
Location / Contacts / Camera / MicrophoneNot requested
All Files AccessNot requested; Android 11+ uses per-file consent for tag edits

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose any information to anyone. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or analytics. There are no third parties involved in the app's operation.

6. Your control and data deletion

  • You can edit or delete playlists inside the app at any time.
  • Uninstalling Rs Music, or clearing its data from Android Settings, permanently removes all locally stored playlists, settings and library state.
  • Exported backup files are ordinary files on your device. Uninstalling the app does not delete them; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.
  • There is no server-side copy of your data, so there is nothing to request or delete from us.

7. Children's privacy

Rs Music does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is suitable for all ages.

8. Security

Your library data stays inside your device's app-private storage. We recommend keeping your device screen locked and updated. Tag editing on Android 11+ uses the system's own per-file approval dialog.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version always applies to the app version listed at the top.
