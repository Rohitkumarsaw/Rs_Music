Built for Speed & Privacy

Engineered Without Compromise

Every feature in RS Music is crafted to respect your battery, storage, and listening flow.

100% Offline & Ad-Free

No INTERNET permission. No banner ads, no telemetry, no accounts—just instant playback of your local audio library anywhere, even on flights.

Smart Library Tabs

Automatically organizes your MP3, FLAC, WAV, and M4A files into clean All Songs, Folders, Playlists, and Favorites tabs.

Background & Bluetooth

Full Android media session integration with rich lock-screen notifications, TWS earbuds gesture support, and automatic pause on headset disconnect.

Built-in Sleep Timer

Fall asleep to your favorite tracks without draining battery all night. Set custom countdown timers to smoothly fade out and stop playback.

Dual OLED Dark & Light

Switch effortlessly between battery-saving true pitch-black AMOLED dark theme and a crisp, high-contrast daylight theme with one tap.

Instant Lightning Search

Filter thousands of songs, artists, albums, or folder paths in real time with zero lag thanks to native Flutter isolate indexing.

Synced Lyrics & Tag Editor

Read time-synced .lrc lyrics, then edit title, artist, album, genre and album art without leaving the app.

Queue, Speed & Backup

Reorder the playback queue, fine-tune speed from 0.5x to 2.0x, skip +/-10s, and back up playlists and settings to local files.
